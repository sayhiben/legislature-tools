# DUP-006 — Add evidence-matrix rendering and explicit disagreement-handling rules

**Priority:** P1  
**Status:** Done (2026-03-01)  
**Depends on:** DUP-005

## Goal
Make disagreement across evidence families visible and interpretable, rather than allowing one analysis family to suppress or overwrite another.

## Scope
Add an analyst-facing interpretation layer that handles combinations such as:
- high VRDB collision anomaly + normal registry similarity
- high registry similarity + normal VRDB collision anomaly
- both high
- both normal but behavioral signals high

Define explicit policy:
- VRDB collision evidence may increase concern, but does not suppress other flags.
- Existing timing/content/metadata evidence may increase concern, but does not erase a VRDB extreme.
- Agreement strengthens confidence in follow-up priority.
- Disagreement narrows the kind of question the evidence is answering.

## Deliverables
- Evidence matrix UI/report section
- Analyst guidance memo
- Tooltip / methodology text for concordant vs discordant signals

## Failure modes and mitigations
- **Failure mode:** users assume disagreement means one method failed.  
  **Mitigation:** each evidence family states its question and allowed interpretation.
- **Failure mode:** dashboards reduce multiple families to a single traffic light.  
  **Mitigation:** no composite score in v1; show separate columns/panels.

## Acceptance criteria
- Reports surface multiple evidence families side by side.
- Analysts can identify disagreement without reading raw tables.
- Interpretation guidance exists for common disagreement patterns.

## Verification
- UX/content review on representative hearings.
- Snapshot tests for evidence-matrix rendering.
- Analyst sign-off on disagreement guidance language.
